# 🧠 Neuroplasticity – How Habits Form in the Brain

**An interactive game that shows how habits physically rewire your brain.**

👉 **Play it here:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/neuroplasticity-game/

---

## What is this?

Every time you repeat something, connections between neurons get stronger. That's **neuroplasticity**, and it's how every habit you have was built.

This website turns that into a game. You build a mini brain and live 100 days as one person. Your goal is good habits instead of endless scrolling.

## ✨ Features

- 🔌 **Build a brain:** drag 5 brain regions into place and watch the connections between them grow
- 🎮 **100-day habit game:** running, scrolling, learning a language, meditating and meeting friends, each with its own energy and time cost
- 📱 **Phone urges:** resist (costs willpower) or give in (strengthens the scroll loop)
- 😴 **Sleep replay:** see how the brain consolidates the day's learning at night
- 🔬 **Live synapse zoom:** action potentials, calcium, neurotransmitter release and new receptors (LTP)
- 🔁 **Habit loop and dopamine:** cue → craving → routine → reward
- 🗺️ **Connectome lab:** your personal wiring diagram as a connectivity matrix
- 🃏 **12 flashcards** on the science, written to be short and ADHD-friendly
- 🪄 **Real habit planner:** build your own habit plan and copy it
- 🏅 **Badges, quiz and autosave**

## 🧪 The science behind it

| Concept | What it means |
|---|---|
| Hebb's rule / LTP | Neurons that fire together, wire together |
| Myelin | Frequently used paths get faster |
| Basal ganglia | Control shifts from the conscious PFC to "autopilot" |
| Dopamine shift | Dopamine moves from the reward to the cue |
| Sleep consolidation | The brain replays the day at night |
| 66 days | The average time for a behavior to become automatic (Lally et al., 2009) |

> ⚠️ This is an educational simplification. Each orb stands for a whole brain region, and the energy bar and learning speeds are game mechanics.
